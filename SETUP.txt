CHANGE LOGIN PASSWORD
After uploading the website files to GitHub, refresh the app and open
Settings > Account > Change password. Enter the current password, a new password
and the same new password again. Choose Save password.
Owner, manager and worker accounts can each change their own password.
This changes the shared login for everyone using that same email account.
No additional Firestore rule changes are needed for this feature.
Passwords are sent to Firebase Authentication; they are not saved in the
app's Firestore database or local storage.

LONGER RECEIPTS AND CUSTOM MESSAGE
Settings > Receipt printer > Receipt spacing: Compact, Roomy, or Extra space.
Roomy is the new default, with more space between items and a bottom paper margin.
Type your message in Message at the bottom of receipts. Enter creates a new line.
The message replaces Thank you!; leave it blank to omit the footer message.
Settings save automatically on this printing device; configure other devices
separately. This affects Order Pad's Print receipt output. Other printing apps
may override the layout. No additional Firestore rules changes are required.

AUTOMATIC DAY CLOSING
Sales close at 00:00 in Asia/Tbilisi (Georgia time), regardless of device time zone.
Keep at least one signed-in device open and online for midnight closing. If all
devices are closed/offline, the app catches up when opened and connected again.
Finished orders from earlier calendar dates are archived
to Days with their full details and totals. New-day orders remain active.
Worker shifts are NOT ended. Late offline orders are archived after they sync.
Unfinished orders stay active until marked done, then join their original day.
Multiple devices can close safely: each archive and its order removals occur in
one transaction. Large days/late arrivals can have multiple entries in Days.
Manual Close day remains available and now uses the same safe transactions.
No new Firestore rule changes are needed if the previous rules are published.
Upload all website files and refresh each device once.

KEEP UNFINISHED ORDERS AFTER MIDNIGHT
Upload all website files, publish the included firestore.rules, and refresh all
devices. At midnight only finished orders are archived. Unfinished orders stay
in Orders with their original date displayed. Mark an earlier order done and
it joins the automatic archive for that original date; its total is added there.
The update happens when online, normally within a few seconds.
Manual Close day now also keeps unfinished orders active.
Very large days may need extra archive parts. Existing historical entries remain
unchanged, including archives made by older releases.

BOLT FOOD CHANNEL
Upload ALL files, including bolt.html, to your existing GitHub Pages folder.
Publish the included firestore.rules, then refresh all devices.
Use Restaurant / Bolt Food tabs at the top to switch channels.
Bolt Food has a separate empty menu (Bolt menu tab), orders, totals and Days
history. Owner and manager can add/edit/remove Bolt menu items. Workers can
enter and manage Bolt orders. No restaurant menu items or orders are copied.
Both channels close finished orders at 00:00 Georgia time. Either channel can
process closing for both while a signed-in device is open and online.
Unfinished orders stay active; when marked done later, they join their original
date in that channel's history. Manual Close day affects the selected channel.
Shifts, payments, login accounts and shared receipt logo remain shared.
Bolt Food receipts are labelled BOLT FOOD ORDER. Orders are entered manually;
there is no automatic connection to the Bolt Food service.
